I Joined a Panel at Black Hills State University

Brian R. Demsey | November 2026

← Back to Articles

On November 5 I joined a panel at Black Hills State University in Rapid City, the opening session of the President's Speaker Series on Civil Discourse and Complex Challenges. The topic was “Energy, Innovation and the Environment,” and the session examined how leaders balance technological advancement, including artificial intelligence, with environmental responsibility.

The evening was moderated by Congressman Dusty Johnson and hosted by the BHSU Center for Civic Engagement. In the photo: me, Congressman Johnson, and BHSU President Steve Elliot.

For decades as an actuary consulting on benefits strategies, and now as a more general strategist, my staff and I have been required to balance the objectives of organizations and recipients. Bringing together parties from different sectors of the community, each with their own agendas, and finding the common ground, is the work I enjoy most.
