I Found the Bug!

What AI Gets Right, What It Gets Wrong, and What We Can Do About It

Brian R. Demsey

She can play mah jong all day.

Ask her to read four lines of computer instructions? Absolutely not.

Brian Demsey is 83. For thirty months he has worked with Claude, an artificial intelligence, nearly every day: building apps, publishing websites, and fixing his own computers. He kept the transcripts.

This is what he found. A machine that always has an answer, even when it is wrong. A logo that took an hour because the AI insisted it could not see a web page. Email that kept going to the wrong mailbox. And the plain rules that finally made the machines reliable.

The book starts at middle C and climbs, chapter by chapter, to how Brian trains AI today. Go as far as you wish. Every chapter ends with a funny story.
